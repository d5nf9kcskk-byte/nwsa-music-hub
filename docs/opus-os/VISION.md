# Opus OS — Vision

**Opus OS** is Grant Gilman’s independent business: an OPAS-class platform for orchestra planning and administration (season/calendar, personnel, library, contracts, musician portal, reporting).

**Product rule (locked 2026-08-12):** This repository — **NWSA Music Hub** — is the codebase that **becomes** Opus OS. NWSA is customer zero. One product lineage. Do not stand up a parallel app.

## Who buys (eventually)

Professional and institutional ensembles that today buy or envy OPAS: connected calendars/services, rosters and assignments, repertoire and library, contracts for extras/soloists, musician-facing access, history and reports.

## What “done for v1” means (directional)

Not full OPAS parity. A credible core that NWSA already runs in production, generalized enough that a second organization is not a rewrite:

- Multi-ensemble schedule + roster + attendance (shipped for NWSA)
- Repertoire library with parts links (Bottega/Editore channel)
- Public + director surfaces
- Clear path to multi-tenant / second org without painting into single-school corners when cheap to stay general

# NWSA Music Hub: what we collect and who sees it

*For parents and guardians of students in the New World School of the Arts music division. En español: [privacy-notice.es.md](privacy-notice.es.md).*

The NWSA Music Hub is the music division's website and app. Students use it to see their schedule, music and assignments, and to fill in forms. Directors and teachers use it to plan rehearsals, take roll and grade. Students do not have accounts. Staff sign in with an approved Google account.

## 1. What anyone can see on the public site

This is limited on purpose. The public site can only read these items:

- **About each student:** name, preferred name, instrument, section, which ensembles and classes they are in, whether they are active, and grade level. Never pronunciation, contact details, attendance, grades or notes.
- **Schedules:** rehearsals, classes and concerts, and short-term roster changes (who is added to or pulled from a rehearsal or concert). The reason for a change is never shown.
- **Private lesson times:** date, start and end time, room, teacher's name, instrument, and whether the lesson is on or cancelled. The lesson's grade, comments and repertoire are not shown.
- **A personal calendar file for each student** (their schedule, including lesson times), seating charts, and the names of students who booked a time on a sign-up (for example, to play in a master class).
- **That a video was turned in** for an assignment, and when. The video itself is not shown on the site, but uploaded video files are not locked behind staff sign-in either.

## 2. What only staff can see

| Information | Why we keep it | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Student email and phone; parent and guardian names, emails and phones | To reach families about rehearsals, absences and grades | Directors; a private-lesson teacher for their own students |
| School-issued student ID, name pronunciation | Matching district lists; saying names right at concerts | Everyone with a Hub staff account, including student assistants |
| Attendance marks and school-day tardies | Taking roll | Directors, classroom teachers, student assistants (tardies: directors) |
| Grades (lessons, assignments, quarter grades) and staff notes | Grading and reporting to the school | Directors; the teacher who gave the grade |
| Concert check-in records: name, school email, time, and a photo taken at the door | Proof of concert attendance, which is part of the grade | Directors (photos are also copied to the directors' Google Drive) |
| Absence reports, and photos of office excuse notes | So a director can approve or decline the absence | Directors and student assistants |
| Signed permission PDFs, including emergency contacts and medical notes | Field trips and other forms the district requires | Directors only |
| Form answers, music copy requests, messages from parents, online test answers, video submission details | To answer the request or grade the work | Directors, and the teacher who runs that form or class |
| Excused-from-a-concert records and their reason | Religious, medical and family excusals | Directors and private-lesson teachers |

## 3. Forms you can fill in without signing in

Students and families can send: an absence report (with an excuse-note photo when leaving early), a sign-up or permission form (some with a signed PDF), a music copy request, a message to the directors, an online test, a playing-exam video, a concert check-in (school email and a photo), and a custom calendar choice (no personal details). Each form accepts only a fixed set of fields. Most also require a student who is already on the roster; the message form and "anyone with the link" forms take a typed name instead.

## 4. Email, security checks and devices

- **Email.** The Hub sends email through a school Gmail account: form confirmations, answers to absence and copy requests, lesson logs, and grade reports. Copies of sent emails are kept in the Hub's database.
- **Security check.** Public pages may run Google reCAPTCHA (through Firebase App Check) to tell real browsers from automated traffic. Google receives information about the browser for that check.
- **Your device.** The site saves a few choices on your own phone or computer, such as the student you saved and your color settings. They never leave the device unless you send them to another one yourself.
- **Phone alerts (staff trial).** Staff can turn on alerts that buzz their phone when something happening today is changed or cancelled. The Hub stores a device token for each phone that turns them on, plus the ensembles it follows. Nobody can read those tokens through the app, and an alert never names a student. Families cannot sign up yet. If that opens later, a family phone that turns alerts on would also store the ids of the students saved on it, and this notice will say so first.
- **Spanish banner drafter (built, switched off).** The Hub has a button that would ask Anthropic's Claude for a Spanish version of a schedule change note a director just wrote, for the director to check before saving. It would send only that note, never a student's name or any other record. It is off today, so nothing is sent, and this notice will say so before it is turned on.

## 5. How long we keep it

- When staff delete or replace a file's record (a document, a photo, a video), the file is deleted too.
- Unfinished video uploads are deleted after about 48 hours.
- **Everything else has no set retention period yet.** Records stay until staff delete them, and students who leave or graduate are marked inactive rather than deleted. The school is deciding a retention schedule, and this notice will be updated when it does.

## 6. Who else handles the data

The Hub runs on Google Firebase (database, file storage, sign-in, server functions) and is published through GitHub. The full list of services, what each one receives, and their privacy terms is in [vendors.md](vendors.md).

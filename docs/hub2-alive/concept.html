    Every page, at once

    Every screen in the Hub is built from about 20 shared parts: cards, rows, buttons, fields, drawers, tabs, titles and empty states. Give those parts the new look and the eight motions, and all of the Hub's screens change together, staff and public, including screens built later.

    2

    The mockups' big pieces, built for real

    The Now card with its live bar, the week strip, Tonight with a countdown and a gold Check in button, the concert hero, instrument badges on every group, drawn empty states, the living mark.

    What you need first sits at the top of each page in one big card. Fewer, larger buttons. Everything you owe in one yellow card.

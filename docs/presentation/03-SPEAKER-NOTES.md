# NWSA Music Hub — Speaker Notes
### The cue-card version. Same notes are inside each slide's notes area in the .pptx.

Use this if you'd rather glance than read. The full wording is in `02-SCRIPT.md`.
`⚠` = something to verify or fill in before presenting.

---

**1 · NWSA Music Hub** — *0:30*
Greet by name. One sentence: why it exists, how you use it, why it's less work.
Promise three question breaks and mean it.
⚠ Names. ⚠ Say "New World School of the Arts" in full.

---

**2 · Three things, then you decide** — *0:30*
Why → How → Why faster. Tell them when you'll be done so they can relax.

---

**3 · The problem** — *2:00*
Every fact had to be entered more than once. Attendance was worst: the old
Notion workspace was fine on a laptop, impossible on a phone in front of an
ensemble — so roll went on paper and got typed later, or never.
⚠ **Your own 30-second story goes here.** First person. Specific. This is the
only emotional beat in the talk; don't skip it.

---

**4 · One place. Two doors.** — *1:30*
Public site: no login, no app, anyone. Director Panel: Google sign-in, invited.
Same data, different door. No export step between them.
Point at the dark "Director Panel — editing area" band if it's on screen.

---

**5 · The rule** — *1:00*
**"You set it once. Everyone sees it."** Say it twice, slowly.
Cancel a rehearsal → red banner + every student schedule + subscribed phone
calendars. One action, no chasing.

---

**6 · What a family sees** — *2:00* — **LIVE**
Home → point at where the red change strip appears (no strip = normal day, and
that's the point) → My Schedule → look up a real student by name.
Site remembers them on that device. Parent mode holds several kids.
Line: *"no account, no password to reset."*

---

**7 · Your day starts here** — *1:30* — **LIVE**
Today screen. Read one real card: time, room, "N expected", and the roll receipt
("not taken yet" / "taken 2:14 · 3 absent").
Below: today's changes, lessons pulling students out, unexcused-absence reminder.
Filter to one ensemble — it remembers.

---

**8 · Take roll: mark only the exceptions** — *3:00* — **LIVE, ON YOUR PHONE**
Do the taps, don't describe them: Absent → Late → Lesson (time window + required
reason) → point at the count chips (they filter) → **Finish roll — summary** →
call/email/copy.
Land it: *"three taps, because three people were out."*
Quiet extras: minutes-late recorded, last-5-rehearsal dots, parent-reported
absences waiting on the row, section-gap warning.

---

**9 · Why that's faster** — *1:30*
Elsewhere work scales with roster size. Here it scales with absences.
80 students, 4 out = 4 taps. Nobody out = zero.
⚠ Use your real roster number or say "about eighty."
⚠ **Say out loud that you have not timed it.** No minutes claims.
Finished in the room = finished.

---

**10 · Roll answers questions later** — *1:30*
Receipt (when + who). Pattern (5 dots). Follow-up queue (Excuse / Contacted /
Dismiss — a queue meant to hit empty). Export to spreadsheet.

---

**⏸ PAUSE 1 — 2:00**
*"Questions on roll before I move on?"*
If silence: name one person, ask one specific question, count to five.

---

**11 · When the day changes** — *2:00*
Schedule Change: swap two blocks · shift a time · move a room · cancel.
Automatically: tagged as changed + red public banner + offer to post a notice.
**Revert to normal** restores the pre-change snapshot and pulls the notice down.
**Close a day** = one button cancels everything on a date + one notice.
⚠ Local phrasing for the closure day.

---

**12 · Subs and pull-outs** — *1:30*
Temporary roster change: add or pull, one event or a date range, reason required.
Permanent roster untouched; expires itself.
While active it's real: "Sub" badge on roll, appears on Who's Out, shows on the
student's public schedule. One entry moves a student both out and in.
Line: *"nobody has to remember to undo anything."*

---

**13 · Announcements** — *2:00*
Per-ensemble or school-wide. Normal / Important / **Urgent** (site-wide banner —
for "call time moved," not "bring your folder").
Pin · expiry date · schedule for later · Spanish field you write yourself.
⚠ **Teams/email: say the honest version.** Unless you have watched one arrive:
*"the app queues it; that last hop is outside the app and I won't promise it yet
— treat the banner as the delivery."*
Rule: announcements are public. Nothing private in one.

---

**14 · Repertoire becomes the program** — *2:00* — **LIVE**
Enter the piece once (formal title, composer + dates, movements, duration,
program notes, recording, score, **a part link per instrument**).
Attach to a concert, set the order → the printed program is generated.
Masthead: **New World School of the Arts** — say we got it wrong once and won't again.
Same entry gives students a **"My part"** button matched to their instrument.
One piece can belong to several ensembles.

---

**15 · Three more, quickly** — *1:30* — 30 seconds each, don't linger
Documents (syllabus/handbook/forms, per ensemble or school-wide).
Assignments & exams (due dates, on student schedules, Google Form for playing exams).
Seating (seat 1 = principal, published; chair-view roll on rehearsal day).

---

**16 · Getting families in** — *1:30*
Hold up the printed QR page. Wall posters, "find my schedule" poster, per-ensemble
posters, cut-apart folder slips.
Short addresses for typers: `/we`, `/jazz`, `/choir`. ⚠ Read them off the kit.
**Subscribe** — one tap, ensemble calendar on the phone, stays current.
Line: *"the difference between checking and knowing."*

---

**⏸ PAUSE 2 — 2:00**
*"Is there something your ensemble does that you didn't see?"*
Go around by name. Write it down where they can see you writing it.

---

**17 · Who can see what** — *2:00* — **the Dean's slide**
Enforced by the database, not by hiding pages.
**Staff only:** contacts, attendance, progress notes, private lessons.
**Public on purpose:** schedules, concerts, repertoire, announcements — and
student names, instruments, grade, ensemble.
Say the trade out loud: names are public *because* lookup-without-an-account
requires it. It's a decision, reversible, costs student accounts.
Announcements are public by design.

---

**18 · Who can do what** — *1:30*
Owner (only one; adds/removes people) · Director (everything but that list) ·
Teacher (own students' private lessons only; conflicts must be acknowledged, then
show as documented pull-outs) · **Personnel Assistant** (roll only, assigned
ensembles, no contacts/grades/notes, every mark stamped with their name).
⚠ Offer it: *"give me a name and I'll set it up."*
Access changes take effect instantly.

---

**19 · Where it lives, what it costs** — *1:00*
No license, no subscription; free hosting tiers **as configured today**.
⚠ Not "free forever." No server, nothing to hand-back-up; rebuilds and refreshes
calendar feeds on a schedule.
Installs to a home screen; opens with a bad connection.
⚠ If untested in a dead-zone room: *"it opens offline; saving needs a signal and
it tells you when a save didn't go through."*

---

**20 · Old way → new way** — *2:00*
**Walk only three rows.** Pick the three that matter to this room.
The argument in one line: *every fact gets entered once, by the person who knows
it, and stops being re-asked.*
Then the honest caveat: it doesn't delete the work, it moves it to the front —
term setup is real; the payoff is ten months of not re-entering.

---

**21 · What I'm asking of you this week** — *1:30*
1. Sign in and look. (⚠ Check the Directors list first so "not authorized" isn't
   a surprise.) 2. Take roll once, for real. 3. Check your month's calendar and
   tell me what's wrong. 4. Name anyone who should take roll for you.
⚠ Commit to a check-back date out loud.

---

**22 · When you're stuck** — *0:30*
**Start Here** in the app menu (answers link straight to the screen; prints).
**Glossary** at the bottom. Then email you. ⚠ Address.

---

**23 · Questions — and what I'm not claiming** — *8:00+*
Read the open items aloud: no measured time savings · Teams/email relay
unverified ⚠ · student names are public by design · maintenance is you ⚠ ·
anything you promised mid-talk.
Then: *"What did I miss, and what's going to break?"* — and stop talking.

## The one idea

> **You set it once. Everyone sees it.**

There is one place where a rehearsal, a cancellation, a concert, a piece of music
or an announcement is entered — and every other place updates itself: the public
site, each student's own schedule, the phone calendars people subscribed to, and
the printed concert program.

**Two doors, one system.**

| | **The public site** | **The Director Panel** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Students, parents, anyone | You, signed in with Google |
| Login | None. No app to install | Google sign-in, invitation only |
| What | Today's schedule, calendar, concerts, their own schedule, sheet music parts, announcements, documents | Everything above, plus the editing side: roll, roster, schedule, repertoire, announcements |

## The six things you'll actually do

**1. Take roll — mark only the exceptions**
Open **Take Roll**, tap the rehearsal, and tap only the students who are
**Absent**, **Late**, **Excused**, or out for a **Lesson**. Everyone else is
present without you touching them. Six people out of eighty means six taps.
Finish with **Finish roll — summary**: who's out, one tap to call or email home,
and a copy-ready list.

**2. Change the day**
**Schedule Change** — swap two blocks, shift a time, move the room, or cancel.
The change posts itself to the public site as a red banner, and **Revert to
normal** puts the original back. **Close a day** cancels everything on one date
and posts one notice.

**3. Move a student temporarily**
**Temporary Roster Changes** — sub a student into your ensemble for one concert,
or pull them for a date range. Always with a reason. **Your permanent roster is
never touched**, and their public schedule updates automatically.

**4. Post an announcement**
School-wide or just your ensemble. Three levels — normal, important, urgent.
You can pin it, give it an expiry date, schedule it to post later, and add a
Spanish translation that families read with the **ES** button.

**5. Build the program**
Add a piece once — title, composer, movements, duration, program notes, and a
part link per instrument. Attach it to a concert, drag the order you want, and
the **printed program prints itself**. Students see *their* part on their own
schedule page.

**6. Everything else, when you want it**
Documents (syllabus, handbook, forms) · Assignments & playing exams with a Google
Form link · Seating charts (seat 1 = principal) · Progress notes · a printable
**QR kit** of posters and folder slips that puts families on the site in one scan.

## Old way → new way

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance | Written down, typed up later | Tapped once, in the room, on your phone |
| "Is rehearsal cancelled?" | Individual replies, all evening | One edit → banner on the site, everyone sees it |
| A student subbing in | Roster edited and remembered back | Temporary, reasoned, self-reversing |
| Concert program | A separate document, retyped | Generated from what you already entered |
| Sheet music parts | Handed out and re-handed out | Linked to the piece; a student finds their own |
| Who's out today | Ask around | One screen: **Who's Out** |
| The calendar | Copied into several places | Entered once; families subscribe to it |

**The efficiency is not that it's a nicer screen.** It's that each fact gets
entered one time, by the person who knows it, and stops being re-asked.

*The "Before" column describes the Notion-based workflow the Hub replaced. No
one has timed the two against each other — the case above is about the shape of
the work, not a measured number of minutes.*

## Who can see what

- **Public** (no login): schedules, concerts, repertoire, announcements, ensemble
  rosters — student **names, instruments, grade and ensemble**, so a student can
  find their own schedule without an account.
- **Staff only** (enforced by the database itself, not just a hidden page):
  contact details for students and guardians, **attendance**, progress notes,
  private lessons.
- **Never** put anything private in an announcement — announcements are public
  by design.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, and is the only one who can add or remove people |
| **Director** | Full editing everywhere except the access list |
| **Teacher** | Schedule private lessons for their own assigned students only |
| **Personnel Assistant** | Take roll for their assigned ensembles — nothing else |

A student aide can take attendance for you without seeing a single phone number.
Every mark is stamped with who made it.
